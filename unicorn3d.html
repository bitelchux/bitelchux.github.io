Rainbow Road

Guide the unicorn through 50 procedurally generated golden stables 🏆

  • 🦄 Move one step at a time with arrow keys / WASD (or the buttons below).
  • 👹👺🦇🐉👻🕷️🦂⚡ Dodge the monsters and storms crossing the clouds — each moves differently, and new ones join in as you level up.
  • 🍬 Hop across the candy platforms to cross the rainbow river without sinking.
  • 🛡️❄️🐌 Grab power-ups on the ground: a shield, a freeze, or a slow-down for enemies.
  • 🥽 With a VR headset: push the controller stick to move step by step.
No headset handy? No problem — the whole game works with a keyboard. To try VR mode without one, install the Chrome extension "Immersive Web Emulator".
