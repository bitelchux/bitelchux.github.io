📖 How to play
🎯 Goal
Walk all 4 sides of each cloud ☁️ to reveal a rainbow!
🕹️ Controls
⌨️ Arrow keys or WASD to move.
📱 On-screen buttons or swipe.
⚡ Power-ups
❄️ Freeze enemies  •  🐢 Slow them down
⭐ Invincible  •  💣 Bomb!
😈 Enemies
Levels 1 to 5 each introduce a new enemy: 😈 imp, 🦂 scorpion, 🐍 snake, 💀 skull and 👻 ghost. From level 6 on they team up, grow in number and get faster. Skulls and ghosts can teleport, and ghosts also phase through clouds.
